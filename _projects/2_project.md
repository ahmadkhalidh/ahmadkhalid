This project investigates the relationship between subword tokenization and cross-lingual representation alignment in multilingual large language models, focusing on English–Hausa and English–Nigerian Pidgin parallel data.

The broader objective is to improve understanding of multilingual representation learning and identify limitations affecting underrepresented languages in large language models.
