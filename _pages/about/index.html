My research interests include trustworthy and multimodal AI, computer vision, and natural language processing, with a focus on developing robust and interpretable systems for healthcare and socially impactful applications. I am particularly interested in medical AI, multilingual language models, low-resource NLP, and vision-language learning. I also served as a Judge at the Deep Learning Indaba Community Challenge in Kigali, Rwanda, evaluating innovative AI solutions from across Africa.

I graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Science from Federal University Lokoja in 2023. My undergraduate research, titled Real-Time Road Anomaly Detection via Masked Autoencoder-Enhanced Vision Transformers, explored the application of self-supervised learning and transformer-based architectures to real-world computer vision problems. Further details are available in the Publications section.
