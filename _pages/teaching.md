## Teaching and Mentoring

From 2019 to 2023, I served as a volunteer academic tutor at Federal University Lokoja, Nigeria, supporting undergraduate students across computer science, mathematics, and physics.

My responsibilities included conducting tutorials, facilitating examination preparation sessions, developing instructional materials, and providing academic guidance to students.

## Teaching Experience

- Supported over 2,500 undergraduate students.
- Conducted more than 80 tutorial sessions.
- Facilitated over 30 examination preparation sessions.
- Received the Tutor of the Year award on two occasions.

## Teaching Philosophy

My approach to teaching emphasizes conceptual understanding, analytical reasoning, and practical problem-solving. I aim to make complex technical concepts accessible through structured explanations, worked examples, and active student participation.

I am particularly interested in supporting students from underrepresented and first-generation backgrounds and fostering an inclusive learning environment that encourages intellectual curiosity, independent thinking, and academic development.
